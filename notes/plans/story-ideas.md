Current ideas for possible story/worldbuilding:
- All the areas in the game are part of one giant research facility / technological device.
- When activated, the device gives the user ultimate control over reality (like the Infinity Gauntlet, or the One Ring, or the Plutonic Engine, or that device in the Talos Principle 2).
- However, the device is also very dangerous to use: when it was used the first time, it shattered the planet into the various floating islands that can be seen in-game.
- As a result of this catastrophe, the device was shut down and defenses were set up to make sure it would never be used again. (This is the explanation for all the enemies/traps/obstacles in the game).
